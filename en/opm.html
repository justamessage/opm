Project
of your life.

There is only one person in your life who matters. And that person is you.

That sounds selfish and is the opposite. What you do not have yourself, you cannot give to anyone — no happiness, no success, no peace. Everything you want for your people starts with you.

Read on

You are not
like the others.

That is not flattery, it is the starting point. Your life has an order — and no trend set it. Your story, your people, your work and your mind did.

That is why no path that is the same for everyone works. Not because some people are better. But because nobody responds to the same words, colours and people — and because what goes first in your life instead of you is something different for everyone.

The same plan, the same rule, the same exercise — four people, four results. Three of them did not try any less.

Heaven Paradise Freedom Peace. Enough. Finally.

Four people, four words, one state they mean: being able to do what you love. Not having to. Not asking first.

These are not places you reach one day. They are states you get into — and fall out of again if you are not careful.

Everyone here is worth the same. Nobody does the same thing to get there.

What goes first
instead of you.

The same sentence from the start, said four different ways. One of them hits you right in the middle, three leave you cold — no matter how clever you think they are.

This is not a personality test and not a box to put you in. It is the reason nine questions are coming up.

Read all four. Three of them are sentences about other people. At one of them your shoulders will tense for a moment.

The people

“Someone else always comes first. What is left over is mine.”

You function. And you take too long to answer when someone asks what you want.

The pace

“My calendar decided. Nobody asked me.”

Everything in your life runs — just at a pace you did not choose.

The opinion

“What they would say weighs more than what I want.”

You have known for months what you want to start. And you do not start.

The work

“Everything is running. I come last on my own list.”

You deliver. You do not lack drive — you lack opponents at eye level.

That is what personal development means here: not a programme everyone goes through, but the question of which of the four places you are stuck at right now — and what helps there.

And one thing
everyone does.

The four rooms separate. This connects: the same daily practice, no matter which room you come from — and it shows that the problem is rarely discipline, but order.

The same in the whole house

Five actions. Every day.

Six days a week, Saturday off. Each action so small that no excuse holds. It is scored in two states: done or not.

Missed days are not made up. Whoever makes up days is negotiating — and the negotiating is the real opponent.
